Ether's Cymatic

Sound you can see as light

Ether's Cymatic is a browser app that turns words, tones, songs and your own voice into glowing cymatic patterns, the shapes sand makes on a vibrating plate and ripples make in a vibrating dish of water.

Open the app tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-s-Cymatic

What you can do

  • Type words and hear them. Every letter becomes a note, and each note reshapes the pattern as it plays.
  • Play pure tones from 20 to 2000 Hz, with quick picks such as 432, 440 and 528 Hz.
  • Upload songs and watch the strongest pitch in the music drive the pattern, with loudness setting the brightness.
  • Use your microphone. Sing, hum or play an instrument and the light follows you.
  • Choose the light. Let each note pick its own colour, or choose any colour of the spectrum, a rainbow, white on black, or a black-on-white negative.
  • Save a still image of a word's pattern, captioned with the word, its frequency and its colour.
  • Record video of a whole song with its sound, ready to share.

How it works

  1. Listen. The browser's audio engine plays the sound and measures its strongest pitch and its loudness about sixty times a second.

  2. Vibrate. A square plate vibrating at a pitch has lines that stay still while the rest moves. Sand gathers on those lines. Higher pitches give more, finer lines. The app solves the plate's standing-wave pattern for the current pitch and draws the still lines as light.

  3. Colour. The pitch is doubled, octave by octave, until it reaches the frequency of visible light. That light's wavelength sets the colour, so a given note always glows the same colour.

A4 = 440 Hz × 2⁴⁰ ≈ 484 THz ≈ 620 nm (orange-red)

Good to know

  • Everything runs in your browser. Songs, your voice and your captures never leave your device.
  • Sound starts only after you press a button. That's a browser rule.
  • Recording works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari. You'll get an MP4 or a WebM depending on the browser.
  • The microphone needs your permission the first time you use it.
