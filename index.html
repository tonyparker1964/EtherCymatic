Pure tone

Dan Winter's idea: tones spaced by the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618 interfere so that their sums and differences land on other tones of the same series (f·φⁿ + f·φⁿ⁺¹ = f·φⁿ⁺²), a cascade he calls constructive or "implosive". The arithmetic is exact and you can see and hear the cascade here. His further claims, about charge implosion, gravity and consciousness, are his own hypotheses and are not established physics.

    Medium

    Each look is a physics simulation. Sand: grains only move where the plate's shaking beats gravity. Water: a shaken surface becomes unstable and grows ripples at half the pitch. Light: bubbles crushed by the sound heat up enough to glow (sonoluminescence).

    An ideal 30 cm × 1 mm brass plate. Near a resonant pitch it shakes hard and the sand gathers fast; between resonances it barely moves and the sand may not stir at all, as on a real plate. Untick for the classic idealised plate, which always responds.

    About Ether's Cymatic

    Colour comes from raising the sound's pitch by whole octaves until it lands in visible light (≈400–790 THz). A4 = 440 Hz × 2⁴⁰ ≈ 484 THz ≈ 620 nm, orange-red.
